Rule & Flow is an independent YouTube channel exploring how simple rules give rise to complex, beautiful, and sometimes surprising behavior through short computational simulations.

Explore the channel ↗

What we create

Short visual experiments in fractals, cellular automata, swarms, physics, and emergent patterns, designed to make a rule visible through motion.

Publishing automation

Rule & Flow Uploader is a privately operated tool used by the channel owner to upload and schedule videos through the YouTube Data API, and read channel statistics through YouTube APIs. It is not a public sign-in service.

How Google data is used

With the owner's authorization, the tool accesses YouTube video metadata, performance metrics, and available analytics solely to operate and evaluate this channel. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Questions or data requests?

Contact the operator via the website repository's issue tracker. Do not post credentials or personal information in public issues.
